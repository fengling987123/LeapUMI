action-conditioned prediction

A world model for manipulation.

Robotic manipulation requires more than reacting to the current image. LeapUMI studies a predictive model that can imagine how the scene evolves under different controls, turning visual foresight into a useful planning signal.

From observation to informed action.

01 / OBSERVE

Understand the scene

Start from visual observations of the robot, objects, and surrounding workspace.

02 / IMAGINE

Predict outcomes

Condition generation on candidate controls to forecast how a manipulation may unfold.

03 / ACT

Choose with foresight

Use predicted futures to support more deliberate, controllable robot behavior.
